Decode mode:
Fast decode: Default mode, one decoding pass on received audio; the fastest option.
Deep decode: Multiple passes with signal subtraction on top of fast decode; more weak signals, longer decoding.
wsjtx Normal multi-core: Use the WSJT-X algorithm library (mfsk-core), split 100-3000 Hz into bands (up to 4, 100 Hz overlap) and decode them in parallel, then merge and deduplicate.
* Note: Deep decode and wsjtx Normal multi-core consume more battery and generate more heat during long monitoring.
* Note: wsjtx Normal multi-core requires arm64-v8a or x86_64 devices; other devices fall back to the original decoder automatically.
